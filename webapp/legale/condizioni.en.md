## 3. What the Service does

POI•LOVE is a **community map of beloved places**. The Service lets you, among other things:

- **discover and create POIs** (points of interest): places on the map with a name, description, category and photos;
- express appreciation for places ("**love**") and follow other people;
- create and share **lists** and **itineraries** of places;
- find **travel companions** and connect with other members of the community;
- use **ILLI**, an assistant based on artificial intelligence, for suggestions about places and areas;
- search for places "**near me**" using your device's location;
- collect **points** and take part in gamification and referral mechanics;
- if you want, take out a **paid subscription** for additional benefits (see section 11);
- write **reviews** of places belonging to people you are connected with: they pass through automatic moderation before appearing (see section 6);
- if you run a business, publish your venue's **opening hours, menu, telephone and payment methods**, and get a **QR code** to print;
- record your **voice** on a place, and listen to the **audio guides** of official places;
- leave **voice messages** on a travel companion group's board, which are also transcribed into text;
- **report** content that is wrong: a report is looked at within twenty-four hours.

The Service keeps evolving: we may add, change or remove features at any time, trying to limit the impact on your experience.
