# POI•LOVE Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 21/08/2026*

> We wrote this policy ourselves, it is not a bought template. It is in force as it stands. If something does not look right to you, write to us: we will correct it.

POI•LOVE is the community map of beloved places: a space where people mark, tell and share the places they care about. To make that work we process some of your personal data. This policy explains, honestly and readably, what data we collect, why, on what legal basis, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what rights you have.

POI•LOVE launches publicly in Albania and is subject to Albanian law no. 124/2024 on the protection of personal data, aligned with EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR). If you are a user in the European Union, the GDPR also applies to you. We process your data in compliance with both.

## 2. What data we collect and why

We collect only the data needed to run the app. Here is the complete list, category by category.

### 2.1 Registration and account data
- **Email** and, for classic registration, a **password** (kept only in encrypted form, never in the clear).
- If you register through an external provider (**Google, Facebook, Apple, X (Twitter) or LinkedIn**), we receive from the provider you choose: email, name or handle, profile picture. If you do not fill in a username, we generate a default one from the first part of your email (the part before the @): you can change it at any time from your profile.

*Why:* to create your account, sign you in, let you recover access and run the service. Without this data we cannot give you an account.

### 2.2 Public profile data
- **Username** (required), **display name**, **avatar**, **bio**, **preferred language**.

*Why:* to identify you publicly in the app, show your profile to other people, and give you a personal address (@username). Name, avatar and bio are optional: you decide what to show.

### 2.3 Location (GPS)
- **Coordinates** (latitude and longitude) when you turn location on, plus any address, city and country derived from those coordinates.

*Why:* to show you places near you, let you create a POI at the exact point where you are, and make "near me" search and navigation to a place work.

Location is one of the most delicate pieces of data we handle, because it can reveal your habits. Therefore:
- we turn it on **only when needed** (when you create or search for a POI, or navigate), never continuously in the background;
- we always ask permission through the browser or the device;
- you can refuse it or withdraw it at any time from the browser or device settings, and from the app settings.

### 2.4 Photos you upload
- **Photos of places (POIs)** and **profile photos** that you choose to upload.

*Why:* to document and illustrate places, and to personalise your profile.

Read **section 4** carefully, on consent and photos: photos can portray people, and there are precise rules and responsibilities here.

The photos you upload are processed and stored through our media server media.poilove.com (see section 5). To reduce risk, when you upload a photo we **remove the EXIF metadata** (which includes precise GPS, date, device model) before publishing it. If you use a photo to create a POI, we may read the GPS position in its metadata for the sole purpose of proposing the point on the map: that data is not republished with the photo.

### 2.5 Content you create
- **POIs** (places you save), **loves and favourites**, **lists**, **travel itineraries** with their stops, **travel companion groups**, and **follow** relationships (who you follow and who follows you).

*Why:* this is the heart of the app. It builds your map, shares places, organises trips and follows other people.

**Free tags on POIs.** When you create a POI you can add free text tags (up to 10). Tags on public POIs are visible to the whole community and are used as autocomplete suggestions, including for people who are not signed in. Do not put personal data, yours or anyone else's, in tags.

**Reporting a missing category.** If you report that a category is missing, we record the term you searched for along with your user identifier, so we can update and curate the platform's categories.

**Transferring and claiming a POI.** If you transfer a POI through a code or claim ownership of a place, we process the identity of the person transferring and of the person receiving (identifiers and public name) and any free text messages you write in the request: this data is visible to the administration in order to handle and validate the operation.

Some of this content may include data about **other people** (for example the members of a travel companion group, or the email of someone you invite). See section 4.4.

### 2.6 Invitations and companion groups
- When you invite someone to a travel companion group, we process the **recipient's email** and an **invitation code** to deliver the link.

*Why:* to let you invite other people. The invited person can accept or ignore. We send a single invitation, with the option not to receive further messages.

### 2.7 Gamification and referrals
- **Points**, the events that generate them (for example creating a POI, giving a love, sharing), and **referrals** (invitations that lead to new sign-ups).

*Why:* to run the points system, recognise your contributions and prevent abuse and fraud. We record only the actions that generate points, not every single click.

### 2.8 Reports and moderation
- When you **report** content or a profile, we record the **report** (what you reported, the reason, the date) and who sent it.
- When we act for moderation purposes, we record the **moderation actions** (for example suspension, block, removal of content) and their reason.

*Why:* to keep the platform safe, handle reports, protect people and comply with legal obligations. We also keep this data as evidence in cases of abuse or dispute.

### 2.9 Support messages and communications from the team
- To assist and inform you, an **administrator or moderator** can send you **direct messages** that appear in your chat with ILLI•AI, clearly marked as written by a **person from the team** (label "Human") and distinct from the assistant's automatic replies. You can **reply**: the conversation (message text, date and time, read status) is visible **only to you and the team**, never to other users.

*Why:* to give you support, answer your questions and tell you about the service (for example news, offers or any prizes connected with the app). Legal basis: **performance of the contract** and our **legitimate interest** in offering you support. We do not use these messages for advertising profiling and you can ask us at any time to stop them.

### 2.10 Paid subscriptions
- If you take out a **subscription** to one of the paid tiers (**Professional, Professional Plus, Supporter, Patron**, and dedicated profiles such as **Influencer**), we process the data needed to manage it: chosen tier, subscription status, activation and renewal or termination dates, and the connected benefits.
- The **payment data** itself (for example card details) is processed directly by the payment provider we use at the time of activation, under its own policy: we do not keep card numbers.

*Why:* to activate and manage the subscription, grant you the tier's benefits, issue any accounting documents and comply with tax and legal obligations.

### 2.11 What we do NOT save
- The **history of address searches** is not kept in our database: it stays in the session.
- **Voice dictation**, if you use it, is converted to text by your device or browser: **we neither record nor keep the audio**.

## 3. Legal bases for processing

Every processing operation has a precise legal basis (art. 13 of law 124/2024, art. 6 GDPR):

| Data | Legal basis |
|------|-------------|
| Email, password, login data | **Performance of the contract**: without it we cannot give you the service |
| Username, name, avatar, bio | **Performance of the contract** and our **legitimate interest** in running the platform and preventing abuse |
| GPS location | **Explicit consent**, which you can withdraw at any time |
| Photos you upload | **Explicit consent** to the upload; for photos portraying people see section 4 |
| Content (POIs, lists, itineraries, follows, companion groups) | **Performance of the contract** |
| Invitation emails to companion groups | Your **consent** to sending, and your declaration that you have the recipient's consent |
| Points, events, referrals | **Legitimate interest** in running gamification and preventing fraud |
| Messages to the AI assistant (ILLI and the administrative copilot) | **Performance of the contract** to provide the feature you asked for, and our **legitimate interest** in running and improving the assistant |
| Reports and moderation actions | **Legitimate interest** in the safety of the platform and of its users, and **legal obligation** where we must keep them or act |
| Paid subscription management data | **Performance of the subscription contract** and **legal obligation** for accounting and tax purposes |
| Aggregated usage statistics | **Legitimate interest**, in anonymous or pseudonymised form |

In short, the bases we rely on are **consent** (for location, photos and invitations), **performance of the contract** (for account, content, AI assistant and subscriptions), **legitimate interest** (for safety, prevention of abuse and fraud, gamification and aggregated statistics) and **legal obligation** (for moderation, accounting and tax where the law requires it).

Where the basis is **consent** or **legitimate interest**, you always have the right to withdraw consent or to object (see section 8).

## 4. Consent, location and photos: read this

This is the most important section for your protection and for other people's.

### 4.1 How consent works
The consent we ask for is **free, informed, specific and unambiguous**: you give it by a clear action (for example by turning on a feature or ticking a box), never by pre-ticked boxes or by silence. We ask for **separate** consents for different things: one for location, one for uploading photos. You can accept one and not the other.

You can **withdraw consent at any time**, and it is as easy as giving it: from the app, browser or device settings. Withdrawal applies to the future and does not make unlawful what we processed before, lawfully, while the consent was active.

### 4.2 Location
We turn on GPS only when you need a feature that requires it, and we tell you. When you close that feature, we stop using it. If you decide to make a list or a companion group public, remember that the connected location can become visible to others: think carefully about what you share.

### 4.3 Photos: what you may and what you must NOT upload
Photos you upload to a POI are, by their nature, meant to be **seen by other people**. Before uploading a photo, read these rules. By uploading a photo you declare that you respect them and you take responsibility for it.

**What you must NOT upload:**
- Photos portraying **recognisable people** (identifiable faces) **without their consent**. This applies to people in the background too, not only the main subject.
- Photos of **minors** without the consent of whoever holds parental responsibility.
- Photos that allow a person to be **recognised or uniquely identified** by their face (biometric data): this kind of processing is forbidden without the explicit and specific consent of the person concerned.
- Photos that infringe someone's **privacy, dignity or rights**, unlawful, offensive or sexually explicit content, or content you do not hold the rights to.
- Documents, number plates, health data or other sensitive information about other people.

**What we recommend:**
- Prefer photos of the **place without recognisable people**: they are the safest for everyone.
- If people are present, make sure you have their consent, or make the faces unrecognisable.

**Your responsibility.** When you upload a photo portraying other people, you are the one responsible, towards those people, for collecting their consent. POI•LOVE provides the tool, but the choice of what to upload is yours. If we receive a report or a legitimate request, we promptly remove the problematic content (see section 8 for reports).

### 4.4 Other people's data in your content
When you invite someone to a travel companion group or include other people in your content, you give us data about them (for example an email address). You must do so only if you have a legitimate basis for sharing it, and you declare that you have. Anyone invited can always refuse and ask for their data to be removed.

### 4.5 Visibility and default settings
We work so that the most prudent settings are the default ones (for example companion groups private to begin with, and the follow graph not freely browsable by anyone). When you choose to make something public, we tell you.

### 5.1 Transfers outside Albania and the European Union
Some suppliers (in particular **OpenAI**, **Anthropic** and **Google**) have servers in the **United States**, and **Supabase** too may have components there. Sending your data there is a transfer to a third country, which the United States, in general, is not considered adequate by either Albania or the EU.

To make that transfer lawful we rely, depending on the case, on:
- **adequate contractual safeguards** (for example standard data protection clauses) offered by those suppliers, and, where Albanian law requires it, on the **prior authorisation of the Authority (Commissioner)**;
- failing such safeguards, your **explicit consent, informed about the risks** of the transfer, for example when you choose to use the ILLI assistant or place searches that need those services.

Transferring data to the United States means that authorities of that country may, in certain cases, access it with protections different from the European and Albanian ones. We try to **minimise** the data sent: for example the AI assistant (OpenAI or Anthropic) receives your messages and the area you are talking about, not your name or your account; and Google Places receives the search text and the area, not your identity. If you do not want certain data to leave the EU/Albania area, you can avoid using the features that rely on those suppliers (the ILLI assistant, the administrative copilot, searches through Google Places). And in any case, as noted above, do not put sensitive data in chats with the AI assistant.

## 5bis. Processing added after July 2026

Since July 2026 the service does a few more things. We list them here because they concern your data.

**Faces in photos.** When you upload a photo of a place, a program on our machine looks for faces and blurs them automatically, with feathered edges. It protects people who ended up in the frame by chance and gave you no consent. If the program cannot do its job, **the photo is not published**: we would rather lose a photo than publish a stranger's face. From the administration panel we can choose whether to blur everyone's faces or only children's. Photos arrive on the server already blurred: the original with faces in the clear is not kept.

**Reviews.** You can write a review of a place belonging to someone you are connected with. Before it appears, the text is read by an artificial intelligence program that checks whether it respects the rules written in the panel (no insults, no other people's data, no advertising). The text of the review is therefore sent to OpenAI or Anthropic, as with the ILLI assistant. If the program is not sure, the review waits and a person looks at it.

**Venue data.** If you run a business, you can publish opening hours, menu, telephone and payment methods. This is data you choose to make public. We also count how many times your card is opened and how many times your QR code is scanned: those numbers are visible only to you, and are not tied to the identity of whoever is looking.

**Voice and video.** You can record your voice on a place and upload a video. They stay on our machine in Germany. Voice messages in travel companion groups are also transcribed into text, and for that the audio file is sent to OpenAI.

**Subscriptions.** If you have a paid tier we keep the expiry date, the payment reference you give us and the history of renewals. We do not process your card data: payment happens outside the platform.

**Reports.** Anyone can report content. A report keeps who made it, what it concerns and the reason, and is looked at within twenty-four hours.

**Email.** Service emails (sign-in links, notices) go out through **AcumbaMail**, which receives your address and the content of the message. Its servers are in **Spain**, European Union.

## 7. How we protect your data (security)

We adopt technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:
- **Row Level Security (RLS)** on the database: each person can reach only the data they are entitled to.
- **API keys kept on the server side**, behind protected functions (Edge Functions): they never travel in the browser and are not exposed to users.
- **HTTPS** on all connections: data travels encrypted.
- **Internal project files blocked** from the public side of the site, so they cannot be reached from outside.
- **Encrypted passwords** and authentication handled by specialised software.
- **Removal of EXIF metadata** from photos before publication.

No system is 100% secure. If a data breach occurred with risk to your rights, we would notify the Authority (Commissioner) within **72 hours** and, in high-risk cases, you too without undue delay, as the law provides.

## 8. Your rights and how to exercise them

You have full control over your data. Under law 124/2024 and the GDPR you can:

- **Access** your data and obtain a copy (the first one is free).
- **Rectify** inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erase** your data and **close your account**: account deletion is in your profile settings, and it erases or anonymises your data. You can also ask us in writing.
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interest, and in particular object absolutely to any direct marketing communication.
- **Restrict** processing in certain cases.
- **Portability**: receive your data in a structured, readable format and, where technically possible, have it transferred to others.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, for location, photos or any processing based on consent.

To exercise these rights, or to report a photo or content concerning you, write to **admin@321.al**. We answer **within 30 days** of the request. Answers are free; for further copies we may ask a reasonable contribution towards costs.

We do not use your data to take **decisions based solely on automated processing** producing legal effects on you or significantly affecting you. Should that change, we will tell you and explain the logic and the consequences.

## 9. Minors

POI•LOVE is not designed for children. To use it, and for processing based on consent, you must be **at least 16 years old**. If you are under 16, you may use it only with the consent of whoever holds parental responsibility.

We do not knowingly collect data from minors below that threshold without the required consent. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor has given us data without the proper consent, write to us at **admin@321.al**: we will promptly remove that data.
